Nutrition Guide

🥗 Eat Healthy, Live Better

Good nutrition is the foundation of a healthy and active lifestyle.

Essential Nutrients

🍗 Protein

Protein helps build and repair muscles.

Sources: Eggs, Chicken, Fish, Paneer, Milk, Soybean.

🍚 Carbohydrates

Carbs provide energy for daily activities.

Sources: Rice, Bread, Oats, Potato, Fruits.

🥑 Healthy Fats

Healthy fats support brain and heart health.

Sources: Nuts, Seeds, Avocado, Olive Oil.

🥦 Vitamins & Minerals

Boost immunity and support body functions.

Sources: Fresh Fruits, Vegetables, Dairy Products.

✅ Do not skip breakfast
✅ Limit sugary drinks and snacks
✅ Avoid excessive junk food
✅ Eat fresh seasonal fruits and veg
