About SportFit

Welcome to SportFit 🏃

SportFit is a complete Sports, Fitness, Workout and Nutrition platform designed to help people live a healthier and more active life. Our goal is to provide workout guidance, fitness knowledge, nutrition awareness and sports learning — all in one place, powered by AI.

What We Provide

Everything You Need to Stay Fit

🏏

Sports Training

Learn sports techniques, improve skills and discover training tips for Cricket, Football, Basketball, Badminton, Tennis and Athletics.

💪

Fitness Programs

Follow fitness plans for beginners, intermediate and advanced users. Cardio, Strength, Yoga and fat-loss programs.

🏋️

Workout Library

Explore 100+ workouts — cardio, strength training, yoga, HIIT and home workouts with detailed explanations.

🥗

Nutrition Guide

Understand healthy eating with meal plans, calorie counters, water trackers and essential nutrient guides.
